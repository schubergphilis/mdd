Line breaks

A line with two trailing spaces
forces a hard break (the markdown two-space form).

A line with a backslash
also forces a hard break (the GFM backslash form).

A paragraph that wraps onto the next source line without trailing spaces becomes a soft break — which Confluence storage flattens to a single line.

Three different ways to break a line, three different round-trips: trailing-space breaks and backslash breaks both round-trip as <br/>; soft breaks collapse to whitespace.
