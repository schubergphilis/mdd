Lazy blockquote continuation

CommonMark allows blockquote continuation lines to omit the leading > marker. The parser folds them into the preceding blockquote paragraph as if the marker had been there. This is the "lazy" continuation form and exists primarily for author convenience.

The first line carries the marker as expected. This second line has no > marker but is folded into the same blockquote paragraph because CommonMark's lazy continuation rule extends the quote until a blank line breaks it. The third line is also lazy and joins the same paragraph.

After a blank line the blockquote ends, and the writer should emit a single <blockquote> element wrapping one paragraph containing all three lines (joined by soft breaks or spaces depending on the writer's wrapping policy).
