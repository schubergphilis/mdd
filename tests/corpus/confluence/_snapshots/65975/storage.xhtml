Multiple inline links in one paragraph

A single paragraph with three external links interleaved with prose. Two of them have text trailing immediately after the closing bracket — the pattern that triggered mdd issues #70 and #71 for ac:link. This fixture exercises the same trailing-text case for ordinary <a href> elements.
