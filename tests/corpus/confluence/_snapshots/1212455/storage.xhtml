Tight list

A CommonMark list is "tight" when no blank lines separate its items. The renderer omits the wrapping <p> inside each <li> — the item's text becomes the direct child of <li>. Confluence storage follows the same convention, so tight lists round-trip as <ul><li>text</li><li>text</li>…</ul> without inner paragraphs.

Tight vs loose is a structural distinction CommonMark makes at the list level, not at the item level: a single blank line anywhere inside the list promotes the entire list to loose. This fixture keeps every item glued so the round-trip should yield a list of plain text <li> children with no nested <p> wrappers.
