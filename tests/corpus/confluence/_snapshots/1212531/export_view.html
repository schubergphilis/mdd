Inline macro — status

The {{confluence:status ...}} inline macro is the markdown-first authoring form of Confluence's status pill. Spec 030 §"Inline macros" pins the curly-brace syntax: {{confluence:<name> key="value" …}} resolves to a <ac:structured-macro ac:name="<name>">… </ac:structured-macro> with one <ac:parameter> per key.

Current project status: ON TRACK. After yesterday's incident the build status flipped briefly to AT RISK before recovering. A failed migration would show BLOCKED until remediation completes.

Inline status pills compose with surrounding inline shapes — bold prose around IN REVIEW keeps the macro intact and the bold span unbroken.
