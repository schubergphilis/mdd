Setext h1 heading

A paragraph after a setext-form h1. CommonMark allows two heading styles: ATX (# prefix) and setext (an underline of = for h1 or - for h2 on the line below the title). Confluence storage uses <h1> / <h2> in either case, so the round-trip should normalise both forms to the same XHTML.

Setext h2 heading

A paragraph after a setext-form h2. Note that setext headings cannot go below level two — there is no ~ underline for h3 in CommonMark. The reader should accept this shape and the writer is free to emit either ATX or setext on the way back; what matters is that the storage representation is identical.
