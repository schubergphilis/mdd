# Tip callout (rich body)

> **Tip:** Tip macros highlight helpful suggestions. They render as a green callout in Confluence.
> 
> 
> 
> The body can contain **bold text**, _emphasis_, and `inline code` like any paragraph.
> 
> 
> 
> Round-trip should preserve the macro wrapper and all inline formatting within the body.
